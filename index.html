How I work

I like problems that fight back — where the constraint isn't "what's possible" but "what's possible by Friday, on this budget, with these tolerances." I move fast from CAD to physical prototype, and I'd rather test a rough version this week than a polished version next month.

Most of my work sits at the intersection of mechanical design, embedded electronics, and manufacturing — I'm as comfortable in SolidWorks as I am soldering a board or running a CNC toolpath.
